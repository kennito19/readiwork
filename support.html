Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a verification take?

Most verifications are completed within minutes. Some background or risk checks may take longer depending on data sources.

Why was my payment successful but no result?

Results may take a short time to process. If there is a delay, contact support with your payment reference.

Can I correct incorrect information?

Yes. Contact support and we will guide you through the correction or dispute process. Some corrections require contacting the original data source (e.g. CRB).

Will this affect my credit score?

No. Personal checks on Readiwork do not negatively affect your credit score.

Disputes & Corrections

If you believe information in your report is incorrect:

  • Contact support with your reference number
  • Provide supporting details
  • We will guide you on next steps

Our Transparency Promise

Readiwork is committed to fairness and transparency. We do not block users for asking questions, requesting corrections, or seeking clarification about their reports.
