Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Readiwork (“Readiwork”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a credit, background, and risk verification platform operating in Kenya. This Privacy Policy explains exactly how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to perform verification services. This may include:

  • National ID number or other government-issued identification
  • Phone number
  • Names and basic identifying details you provide
  • Verification request details (e.g. credit check, tenant screening)
  • Payment confirmation details (transaction reference only — we do not store card or PIN data)
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, and device information for security purposes

We do not collect: passwords to third-party services, bank PINs, or M-Pesa PINs.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To perform the verification service you requested
  • To validate identity and prevent fraud
  • To process and confirm payments
  • To generate and deliver verification reports
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • To improve platform security and reliability

We do not use your data for advertising or resale.

3. Sources of Verification Data

Verification results may be obtained from licensed and lawful third-party data providers, which may include:

  • Credit Reference Bureaus (CRBs)
  • Background verification databases
  • Public and legally accessible records
  • Risk and fraud intelligence partners

Readiwork does not alter third-party data and cannot guarantee the completeness of external records.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on one or more of the following legal grounds:

  • Your explicit consent when submitting a verification request
  • Performance of a contract (providing the requested service)
  • Compliance with Kenyan laws and regulations
  • Legitimate interest in fraud prevention and platform security

5. Data Protection & Security

We take data protection seriously and implement appropriate safeguards, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Restricted access to sensitive information
  • Secure server environments
  • Continuous monitoring for unauthorized access

While no system is 100% secure, we actively work to protect your data from misuse, loss, or unauthorized disclosure.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to:

  • Complete the requested verification
  • Resolve disputes or support inquiries
  • Meet legal, regulatory, or audit requirements

When data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data.

Information may be shared only with:

  • Licensed verification and data providers
  • Payment processors (transaction confirmation only)
  • Regulatory or law enforcement authorities when legally required

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal limits)
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

Requests can be made by contacting us using the details below.

9. Children’s Privacy

Readiwork services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
